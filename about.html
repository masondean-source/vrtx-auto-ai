About Vertex Auto Solutions

Vertex Auto Solutions was founded to modernize communication for car dealerships. Our mission is to improve response times, increase booked appointments, and reduce operational costs using AI-powered automation.

We are currently in a pilot phase, working directly with dealerships to test and refine our systems. All messaging is sent responsibly to customers who have opted in via dealership websites, lead forms, or service scheduling tools.

Customers can opt out of SMS communication at any time by replying STOP.
